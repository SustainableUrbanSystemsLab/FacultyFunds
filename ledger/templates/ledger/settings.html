Person types: fringe and tuition

The defaults come from Georgia Tech's FY2025 schedules: fringe is 31.7% for full benefits, 23.9% for limited benefits, 7.9% for graduate student health insurance, and 1.5% for partial benefits. Tuition remission (GSTRP) is $1,596 per month. Confirm the current rates with your grants office and update them here. Projections use these for anyone without an override on their appointment.

Budget categories

“Subject to F&A” marks categories in the MTDC base that F&A is charged on. Equipment, tuition remission, and participant support are normally excluded. Built-in categories can be renamed but not deleted.

Category rules

Imported rows get a category from the first rule whose keywords appear in the file's category or account column, then in the description. Lower priority numbers are checked first. Separate keywords with commas. End a keyword with * to match the start of a word, e.g. 5161*. Saving re-checks uncategorized transactions.
